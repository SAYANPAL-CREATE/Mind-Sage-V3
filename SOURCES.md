## Student wellbeing design note

Original, education-stage-specific questionnaires were drafted for the website. They are **not** presented as validated clinical instruments. Parent/guardian answers reflect observed child behaviours; they cannot replace clinical interviews or child self-report when clinically appropriate.

Indian public services currently referenced in the website include:

- Tele-MANAS: https://telemanas.mohfw.gov.in/ (14416)
- Child Helpline: https://www.spniwcd.wcd.gov.in/ (1098)
- Emergency Response Support System: https://112.gov.in/ (112)
